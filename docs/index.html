This site collects notes, code, and results for the background model project.

Overview

  • Derivations (short version): Mathematical justifications for EBPMF-BG updates.
  • Implementation: EBPMF-BG algorithm implementation, based on results derived in Derivations (short version),
  • EBPMF-BG on a “Easy” Simulated Example: Showcase EBPMF-BG on a toy example following EBPMF-BG model assumptions.
  • EBPMF-BG on newsgroups: On newsgroups dataset, compare fasttopics and EBPMF-BG performance.
  • Gamma priors: Visualize different choices of gamma priors.

Helpful R Files

  • bg_post.R: referred to in bg_sim and bg_news. For plotting, factorization results analysis.
  • newsgroups.R: Similar to EBPMF-BG on newsgroups, compare fasttopics and bg model runs on fasttopics.

Experiments

  • Impact of bad newsgroups dataset sampling: On newsgroups dataset, compare fasttopics and EBPMF-BG performance working with a subset of newsgroups.

Outdated

  • EM notes: Poisson nonnegative matrix factorization and EM derivations.
  • EBPMF-gamma: Messy work on EBPMF with initial 1 gammamix priors on F, contains some efforts in GTEx data processing.
